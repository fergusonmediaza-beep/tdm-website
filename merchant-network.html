Merchant Network

Point-of-sale deployment and merchant distribution in township communities.

Merchant distribution

Building merchant networks on the ground

TDM deploys point-of-sale (POS) devices to township merchants through a technology and distribution relationship with PayPoint, the POS technology provider. Our local operational relationships help get devices into the communities where we work.

PayPoint is a separate company. TDM does not own PayPoint.
