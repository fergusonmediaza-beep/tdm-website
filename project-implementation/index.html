Project Implementation

Your partner in project implementation & delivery.

PMO service

A project management office on the ground

TDM provides a project management office (PMO) service for organisations delivering projects and programmes in township communities. We recruit, train and deploy field teams, manage implementation at scale, monitor performance and quality, and report on outcomes and deliverables.

We take full accountability for execution and delivery. The organisation that commissions a project sets the objectives; we are responsible for delivering the results, not simply supplying people.

What you can expect

  • A named project lead and agreed responsibilities
  • Field teams recruited, trained and deployed by TDM
  • Performance and quality monitored against agreed standards
  • Regular progress reporting against agreed measures
  • On-the-ground site verification and data capture for business and government clients

Delivery process

How we deliver

  1. 01

    Plan

    Agree scope, communities, timelines, owners and the measures of success.

  2. 02

    Mobilise

    Recruit, train and deploy field teams, and confirm suppliers and readiness before work starts.

  3. 03

    Implement

    Coordinate and supervise the agreed work in each community.

  4. 04

    Monitor

    Track performance against quality standards, and act on issues as they arise.

  5. 05

    Report

    Reconcile records and report outcomes accurately at close-out.
