Track Record

Results from the programmes we support, with their sources.

Gauteng Department of Social Development

Sustainable Livelihood Programme

A programme for unemployed women in Gauteng that took participants from training into running their own food-cart businesses. Programme period: November 2025 to July 2026.

Programme results at close-out

270

asset trackers deployed

Training ran in community venues from 17 March to 23 April 2026, across three modules: food safety and hygiene, life skills, and micro-enterprise business skills. From the 270 women trained, 135 received food carts to start trading, each with a point-of-sale (POS) device and an asset tracker. 125 of the POS devices are currently active. Our merchant network

Communities: Soweto, Diepkloof, Tladi, Moletsane, Orange Farm, Thokoza, Katlehong, Sebokeng, Evaton and Evaton West. Traders often move between locations as business opportunities change.

Who delivered what

  • The programme was delivered by Empire Partner Foundation (EPF) for the Gauteng Department of Social Development.
  • TDM works hand in hand with EPF, supporting the food-cart merchants on the ground: POS device support and troubleshooting, VAS activation, and ongoing support through dedicated WhatsApp groups.
