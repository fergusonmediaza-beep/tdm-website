About TDM

Project implementation and market access in township communities.

Who we are

Township Development Model (TDM)

Township Development Model (TDM) is a South African project implementation and market access company. Through our established local operational network, we reach, engage and serve township and underserved communities that large organisations often find hard to reach.

We work as both a project management office (PMO) and a distribution business. Our project management lets us set up, run and scale distribution networks, and those networks create lasting commercial partnerships.

We build opportunity inside the communities we work in. We provide on-the-job training, in-field coaching and upskilling, so that local people can grow into field agents and entrepreneurs.

We are active in 10 communities across three Gauteng regions, and we act as an implementation partner for programmes delivered in those communities.

Our mission

We work to make efficient and effective use of technology to create self-employment and to develop a social-value marketplace in our townships.
